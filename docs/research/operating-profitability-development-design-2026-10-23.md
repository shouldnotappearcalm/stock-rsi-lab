## Hypothesis, alternatives and boundary

A source-reviewed Chinese operating-profitability proxy measures current annual
consolidated revenue minus cost of sales, selling and administrative expenses,
divided by same-edition explicitly dated prior-year-end total assets. Separately
reported R&D is evidence, not another subtraction or add-back. The mechanism is
operating earning power relative to committed assets, a quality/persistence
hypothesis already motivated in the frozen source-feasibility literature review.
Chinese evidence is mixed; this is a falsifiable candidate, not a published
anomaly replication or a promised return.

Use one top-decile primary with95% equal weights, monthly prior-close formation
and next-session open execution. Eligible equal weight and bottom-decile scores
are descriptive controls only and cannot be promoted. Alternative broad parameter
search or combining price/quality signals is rejected for this experiment: those
would introduce unregistered extra hypotheses. Retaining records without an
experiment would not advance the return objective. A passing development screen
requires subsequent raw-share/source/account confirmation and unchanged2026
continuation; no objective-completion claim follows from normalized Qlib returns.
