## What the primary literature supports and does not

[Sloan1996 author abstract](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2598)
provides an earnings-persistence/earnings-fixation hypothesis involving cash and
accrual components. Only the author abstract/venue identity was refreshed here;
no claim of reproducing the paper's complete methods or U.S. evidence.

[Ball et al.2016 publisher page](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X16300307)
supports a cash-based operating profitability hypothesis. The search index for
the [author-hosted PDF](https://faculty.tuck.dartmouth.edu/images/uploads/faculty/joseph-gerakos/Ball%2C_Gerakos%2C_Linnainmaa%2C_et_al._2016.pdf)
contains a printed-page44 appendix passage describing revenue, cost, selling/
general/admin expenses, R&D treatment, operating working-capital changes and
lagged assets. A direct open returned404: the full appendix was not independently
retrieved or archived in this lookup. These are field-requirement leads to verify
against a recoverable original, not an implemented financial mapping. Do not
assume reported net income minus totalCFO or the tested gross-profit/assets
ratio reproduces the paper. Full empirical replication remains unperformed.

[Du,Wang,Wei2020, publisher's primary article](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0927538X19306870)
reports materially different evidence in China: operating profitability subsumes
cash-based profitability in their tests; their portfolio analysis does not find
significant hedge returns for the three profitability-related measures. This
counsels against assuming a U.S. signal transfers to A-shares or meets this goal.
The [PolyU author manuscript](https://ira.lib.polyu.edu.hk/bitstream/10397/90052/1/PBFJ_Accrual_PolyU.pdf)
exists, but its full empirical tables were not read here. Sample/country and
long-short-versus-long-only limitations cannot be dropped from a future protocol.
