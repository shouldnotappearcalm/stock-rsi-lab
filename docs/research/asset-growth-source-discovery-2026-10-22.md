request and26 already-audited files:all1236 annual cases,the24 reconstructed
monthly source views and historical membership intervals. It reads source
identities,explicit fiscal dates/column roles and industry/source statuses,
without computing an asset-growth value,reading a price feature or scoring a
security. All26 source hashes and the parent request are unchanged afterward.

Among the full394 source cohort,132–140 issuers per monthly view have both
current and explicitly dated priorDecember31 observations from the same latest
annual edition,within2021nonfinancial source labels. Intersecting the initial
cohort with formation-date memberships leaves110–137. Undated generic openings,
explicitJanuary1 balances,unsupported current reports,ambiguous/latest identity

There is no comparative backfill from a later report or different edition.

This underestimates available balance-sheet data:the existing GP parser admits
a case only if revenue,cost and total assets all parse. A failed revenue/cost
table does not establish that its balance-sheet total-assets cell is unavailable.
Choosing only those GP successes would unnecessarily inherit its reporting-
convention selection bias. No completeness or contemporary-sector claim follows
from the110–137 count; no threshold was selected from returns.
