## Separate reconstruction

The standard-library checker imports no production selector, asset parser,
account engine or scorer. It independently reconstructs publication-day-end
and arrival clocks, old catalog availability, fiscal/publication ordering,
same-day conflicts, deterministic witnesses, case/report identities, explicit
periods and all query/terminal summaries. It verifies the complete original
parent output inventories and all consumed before/after hashes. All 72 source
output files remain unchanged through QA.

Seven semantic controls were rejected without an artifact-hash gate: a wrong
query clock, an omitted issuer, a forged industry label, an exposed monetary
field, a promoted/backfilled failed edition, an invented/erased prior year-end,
and a future 2026 edition inserted at the December 2025 formation close.
This is same-researcher separate code after the source run, not blinded review
or evidence of actual historical feed arrival.

The final local full suite passed all 1,209 tests in 18.748 seconds, including
the new source-visibility, runner-interruption/parity and separate-checker
regressions. The source views executed once; the separate actual audit passed
on its first full run.
