## Economic hypothesis and alternatives

The existing inventory lists accounting accruals as a literature lead, not a
run candidate. Sloan(1996), The Accounting Review71(3),289–315, motivates
separating accruals from cash. Its original balance-sheet formula is not
net-income-minus-CFO. The author-uploaded Collins/Hribar September10,2000
working paper explicitly defines a total cash-flow accrual measure as earnings
before extraordinary/discontinued items minus continuing operating cash flow.
The published2002 DOI is10.1111/1475-679X.00041; its publisher page was403,
so do not claim the downloaded working paper is the final published version.

Choices: resume source recovery for already failed low-OP; implement original
Sloan balance-sheet accruals requiring unverified tax/depreciation mappings; or
build a separately named Chinese consolidated cash-flow accrual proxy using
same-edition net profit, CFO and assets. Choose the third as a research decision:
fewer ambiguous cross-statement mappings and direct cash-flow evidence. It is
not a Sloan/Hribar replication or Ball2016cash operating profitability.

Predeclared later candidate: low ascending (consolidated total net profit minus
consolidated net operating cash flow)/mean(beginning,end total assets), bottom
decile, .95equal monthly target, existing development clock/cost controls.
No positive-profit, price, ticker, industry or new exit filter is inferred from
the failed candidate. High-accrual and eligible equal weight are nonpromotable
controls. This is one new adaptive trial within accounting-information research,
not independent confirmation. This source stage does not calculate scores,

CAS consolidated net profit/CFO do not reproduce US continuing earnings/CFO;
