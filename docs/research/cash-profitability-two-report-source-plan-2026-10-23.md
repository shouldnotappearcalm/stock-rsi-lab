# Cash profitability: two-report source inspection

This is a source-mapping inspection, not a strategy registration. The choice to
inspect the original two SH600166 reports is the implementer's bounded research
choice under the standing authorization to continue. It reuses the existing
first-issuer pilot; no issuer is selected by profitability or later returns.

Before reading additional financial cells, bind this plan, the inspection method,
the original capture identities, both annual PDFs, and the retrieved literature

Commit and push that freeze, verify the exact remote head, then execute once in

## Literature and intended interpretation

[Ball et al.2016, published version hosted by UCLA](https://www.anderson.ucla.edu/documents/sites/about/clubs/asam/6-Accruals-cash-flows-and-operating-profitability.pdf),
physical pages4/17, defines amounts first and scales once by prior-year assets.
The operating numerator removes reported selling/general/admin costs from gross
profit. Its R&D adjustment reverses a Compustat-specific reclassification. Cash
conversion uses yearly operating working-capital changes; depreciation in the
balance-sheet accrual measure is a level. Reported totalCFO is a different measure.

[Du et al., author manuscript](https://ira.lib.polyu.edu.hk/bitstream/10397/90052/1/PBFJ_Accrual_PolyU.pdf),
physical pages12/15, has ambiguous denominator/units and depreciation notation.
Its Chinese database identifiers are mapping leads, not sufficient authority to
equate Chinese statement labels with the U.S. variables. The archived manuscript
is dated2021-03-30; the journal article is2020. This is no claim about the
publisher's final appendix. Preserve the failed Dartmouth2016 retrieval separately.

## Fixed inspection and outputs

Only two archived annual PDFs are admitted, fiscal2023/published2024-04-27 and
fiscal2024/published2025-04-26. Record current and comparative columns within
each edition. No2026 financial reports, market prices, ranking or account APIs.

Use pypdf6.10.0 to preserve all physical-page texts and build a label/page index;
use pdfminer20251230 independently on the fixed statement windows104-113 and
98-107, plus the already known2024-report reclassification page156. The latter
windows are inspection aids, not assertions that every page is consolidated.
Review scope, units, fiscal headings and arithmetic from rendered originals
before admitting any numeric factor. Save both extraction views even if they
disagree. No stock selection, ratio or factor value is computed by this audit.

Review revenue, COGS, selling/admin/R&D expenses, assets, current assets/cash,
current liabilities/current debt, income-tax payables, depreciation/amortization,
receivables, inventory, prepayments, operating deferred revenue, trade payables
and accrued expenses. Headings alone do not establish accounting equivalence.
Blank fields remain unsupported; no automatic missing-to-zero convention.

Emit a manifest with before/after input hashes, source page counts, both-reader
statement evidence identities and a label index. A separate manual report must
distinguish numeric row visibility from unresolved economic mapping. Notes can
require a separately bounded continuation; do not silently expand extraction.
