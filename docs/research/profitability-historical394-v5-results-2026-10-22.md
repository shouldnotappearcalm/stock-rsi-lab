## What changed and what remains

and extracted-text hashes and bounded excerpts. Explicit report-preparation or
presentation currency can now combine with a separately explicit local table
unit. Currency-only evidence carries no invented unit. Local CNY unit aliases
support inline preparer text, currency before the unit and parenthesized monetary
unit. Bare integer or Chinese-section/slash note markers require an explicit
note header. Functional currency alone, foreign currency, overlong/cross-page
declarations, extra money cells and financial proxies remain unsupported.
