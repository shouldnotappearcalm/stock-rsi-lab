# Price-delay: local minute-source admission

The candidate remains the original frozen `price_delay_d1`. This phase uses
only 2024–2025 source data and computes no portfolio return. The unchanged
24 monthly target sets, daily raw bars and 378 compiled company actions are
inputs; no outcome-dependent target, source or evaluator change is allowed.

Authenticate the separately frozen capture plan and request, all method and
input hashes, the actual capture manifest, immutable helper snapshot, separate
`price-delay-scope.json`, client cleanup and independent technical capture
audit. Preserve the helper's legacy LOW_BETA60 marker; it does not establish
the identity of this candidate. Require the complete price-delay scope.

Require exactly 2,502 active stock/session identities, 1,584 byte-identical
cache copies and 918 one-attempt fresh request/reservation/response chains.
Reject extra, orphan, modified or future identities and any second attempt.
Preserve the two halted intervals separately; they authorize no synthetic
price or assumption about actual record-date ownership.

Use the existing `inspect_packet` rules without changing the evaluator:
ordinary active day and correct instrument; 240 explicit sampled minute
clocks; source sequence; closing price and positive-volume prices consistent
with original raw daily bars; nonnegative integer conservative volume bounds
within original daily volume; 48 complete five-minute windows. Preserve the
exact query-file hash inventory, source errors, rejected packets and reasons.

Admission requires all 2,502 samples to pass, a complete capture without an
abnormal exit or three-failure stop, exactly 918 fresh reservations/responses,
and one initialized source client closed without error. Otherwise preserve
the full exception inventory and fail. No implicit retry or replacement of a
rejected sample is permitted. Bind the plan to its intended output directory
and preserve any existing output.

This admits reconstructed execution engineering only. Original arrival time,
the SDK's historical date echo, exact share-volume precision, official queues,
market impact, full company-action completeness and actual FIFO tax remain
unverified. There is no new SDK client or network call in this phase.

After admission and independent reconstruction, freeze a separate account
experiment before computing returns: the same CNY 1 million continuous
account, fixed 1%/5%/10% participation and 20/40 bp extra-cost profiles. All
three 40 bp profiles must retain positive 2024 and 2025 returns and at least
90% mean exposure before a separately frozen 2026 diagnostic is considered.
The user's complete objective additionally requires 2026 January–October
return of at least 10%; source admission proves none of these return claims.
